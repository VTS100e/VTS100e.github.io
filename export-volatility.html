Trade Policy Uncertainty & Export Volatility

[cite: 22]
Methodology: VECM-GARCH Cointegration Matrix
[cite: 23]

Research Objective: Assess and track the long-run structural damage of global policy uncertainty indexes transmitted straight into emerging market export output vectors[cite: 23, 24].

Co-integrating Regimes

Using a **Vector Error Correction Model (VECM)** integrated with an underlying GARCH framing, the analysis successfully handles system non-stationarity while extracting conditional volatility behaviors over multi-year macroeconomic cycles[cite: 23].

Key Methodological Insights

  • Analyzed over **128 monthly structural observations** assessing cross-border transmission velocity[cite: 23].
  • Proved a 1-unit escalation in foreign policy uncertainty registers a long-run contraction of **~52 output units** in sovereign exports ($\beta = -51.93, p < 0.001$)[cite: 24].
  • Identified system corrections crawl at a slow **1.68% per month** Error Correction speed, with near **IGARCH persistence** ($\alpha + \beta = 0.94$), proving policy shocks foster highly persistent, sticky risk environments[cite: 24, 25].
