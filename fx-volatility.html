Continuous-Time FX Volatility Modeling

Framework: Extended Ornstein-Uhlenbeck SDE (Bounded MLE)

Research Objective: Mathematically map, evaluate, and rank sovereign ASEAN-5 currency sensitivity reacting to external US monetary policy adjustments.

SDE Framework

Traditional discrete variance systems fail to track intra-day capital adjustments. By designing an extended continuous **Ornstein-Uhlenbeck stochastic differential equation**, the system forces the US 10-year Treasury yield into the model as an exogenous drift parameter.

Key Methodological Insights

  • Estimated continuous parameters across regional datasets utilizing bounded Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE).
  • Ranked ASEAN regional currencies by direct exposure; IDR exhibited the highest structural transmission factor.
  • Proved IDR is over 4x more volatile than SGD (under monetary shocks, outlining critical vulnerabilities for regional carry trades.
