Research Objective: Strictly isolate pure geopolitical capital flight shocks from endogenous market self-excitation tendencies across interconnected international financial systems — distinguishing structural contagion pathways from noise without collapsing the causal architecture.

§ 01 — Model Architecture

Econometric Architecture

By implementing a multi-variate Hawkes process paired with structural jump variables, the mathematical engine successfully monitors real-time feedback properties without processing endogenous system noise as external contagion shocks. The bivariate specification permits simultaneous tracking of cross-market excitation pathways while preserving the parametric separation necessary for causal inference under geopolitical stress regimes.

The jump-diffusion coupling allows the model to discriminate between self-exciting volatility clusters — a naturally endogenous market phenomenon — and exogenous geopolitical shocks that trigger structural regime transitions in EMDE capital flow architectures.

Methodology

  • Engineered framework processing over 15 years of institutional BIS pricing data to ensure temporal resilience across multiple geopolitical stress cycles and macroeconomic regime transitions.
